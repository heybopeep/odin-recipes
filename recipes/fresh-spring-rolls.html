Quick and Easy Fresh Spring Rolls

Fresh spring rolls are a healthy and quick to put together. Let's get started!

Ingredients

  • Roasted pork, shredded
  • Steamed prawns, seasoned lightly with salt
  • Salad leafy greens
  • Fresh cilantro
  • Hoisin sauce
  • Roasted peanuts, crushed
  • Rice paper sheets

Steps

  1. Soak the rice paper sheets in water
  2. Take out the rice paper sheets, and spread a layer of hoisin sauce on it
  3. Assemble the ingredients on top
  4. Fold the edges of the rice paper sheets inwards, and begin to roll it tightly and gently without breaking the paper sheet

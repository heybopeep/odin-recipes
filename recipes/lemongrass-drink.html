5-Min Refreshing Lemongrass Drink

A healthy and refreshing drink, perfect the summer!

Ingredients

  • Lemongrass stalks
  • Honey
  • Water

Steps

  1. Using the back of a knife, bruise the lemongrass stalks
  2. Bring a pot of water to boil
  3. Add the lemongrass stalks into it, and boil for 20 mins until fragrant
  4. Let the lemongrass brew cool down, then serve with ice cubes and honey to your liking
